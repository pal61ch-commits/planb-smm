# Plan B homepage: Signal Field

Status: **Self-authored under explicit creative delegation**.

The owner rejected the first case-page pilot as too narrow and explicitly asked
for the Plan B homepage itself: a beautiful deep background, restrained
animation, and no decorative clutter. Production stays unchanged until review.

## Eight topics

1. **Vibe**
   - Deep, controlled, technical and premium. Plan B black and yellow remain
     the whole identity.
2. **Scroll journey**
   - Signal field, entry routes, one operating system, primary service, work
     scenarios, process, products, proof of responsibility, FAQ and contact.
3. **Energy curve**
   - Strong opening, calm light relief, dark operating depth, clear service
     focus, factual close.
4. **Feeling and remembered moment**
   - The visitor should feel that Plan B turns scattered Avito activity into a
     controlled channel. The remembered moment is the three-plane signal system
     in the hero.
5. **Unique behavior**
   - One yellow pulse travels through three spatial planes: demand, listings
     and enquiries. The page progress uses the same signal color.
6. **Aesthetic range**
   - Premium industrial interface, not a dashboard, not a sci-fi game and not a
     collection of floating marketing cards.
7. **World structure**
   - One continuous dark world with two warm editorial relief sections.
8. **Existing assets**
   - Canonical Plan B logo only. No generated imagery, unverified claims or
     client screenshots.

## Belief to install

Plan B manages the entire route from demand to enquiry, with a clear plan and
without promises before diagnosis.

Motion is slow and structural: one ambient field, one signal pulse, one subtle
pointer tilt and entry reveals. Touch disables tilt. Reduced motion holds the
