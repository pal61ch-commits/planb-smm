# Candidate plan

## Grammar

Continuous agency signal field with editorial relief.

The homepage is not a long-form case and does not need pinned chapters or
scrubbed media. A semantic document stays in normal flow while Scrollcraft
provides entry reveals, a small parallax layer, pointer depth and motion-safe
progress.

## Signature move

The hero contains three translucent spatial planes connected by one yellow
signal: **Спрос → Объявления → Обращения**. It represents the real service
chain and contains no invented statistics.

## Devices

- Scrollcraft flow act for the hero.
- data-sc-in reveals for content that enters the viewport.
- data-sc-stagger for related rows.
- data-sc-tilt and data-sc-spotlight on the hero system, desktop only.
- One low-rate data-sc-parallax wrapper.
- Page-local progress and signal-state readback.

## Performance ceiling

No generated media, video, WebGL or external animation library. The complete
Scrollcraft and page-specific CSS/JS payload must stay below 35 KB gzip.

## Fingerprint gate

The registry is empty. Do not append a shipped row until the owner approves and
the homepage is published.
